About Hofstra Rhodes Scholar
OUR STORY

Hofstra University has a long tradition of combining academic excellence with real-world experiences. Our community is guided by a shared commitment to discovery, inclusion, and impact. Learn about who we are, what we do, and how we help students turn passion into purpose.

Our Mission

To educate leaders who transform communities through research, creativity, and civic engagement. We foster a campus where curiosity is supported by rigorous scholarship, interdisciplinary collaboration, and experiential learning.

Our Vision

To be a national model for student-centered education that advances knowledge and promotes equity, sustainability, and global citizenship.

Our History

Since our founding, Hofstra has continually adapted to serve students and society. From pioneering academic programs to hands-on community partnerships, our milestones reflect a commitment to growth and impact.

1935

Founded with a small, ambitious cohort of students and faculty.

1963

Campus expansion and new research initiatives launched.

2025

Recent achievements include sustainability projects and interdisciplinary centers.
